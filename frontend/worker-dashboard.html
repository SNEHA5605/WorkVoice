How WorkVoice AI Works

Follow these simple steps to report an issue.

1

Raise a Complaint

Select the appropriate category, priority and describe your issue.

2

Owner Reviews It

The complaint is made available to the owner responsible for your workplace.

3

Issue Is Addressed

The owner can review, respond to and update the complaint.

4

Escalation

Complaints that remain unresolved can later be escalated for authority review.

Important

Please provide accurate information when submitting a complaint.

WorkVoice AI is designed to provide workers with a digital channel to report and track workplace concerns. Emergency situations should be reported through the appropriate emergency services or responsible personnel.
