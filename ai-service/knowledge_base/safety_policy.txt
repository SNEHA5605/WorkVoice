WorkVoice Workplace Safety Policy

Workers can report unsafe conditions at their workplace.

Common safety-related issues include:
- Missing safety equipment
- Damaged safety equipment
- Unsafe working conditions
- Missing protective equipment
- Dangerous work areas
- Safety hazards
- Lack of required safety precautions

Workers should describe the safety problem clearly when submitting a complaint.

Safety complaints should be reviewed promptly by the responsible owner or supervisor.

If a dangerous condition presents an immediate risk, workers should avoid unnecessary exposure to the danger and inform the responsible supervisor immediately.

Workers can track the complaint through WorkVoice.

If the complaint remains unresolved and becomes overdue, the worker can escalate it through WorkVoice.
