WorkVoice General Workplace Support Policy

WorkVoice allows workers to report problems related to their workplace.

Common workplace complaints include:
- Lack of equipment or tools
- Accommodation problems
- Water or food problems
- Excessive working hours
- Supervisor-related problems
- Workplace infrastructure problems
- Construction delays
- Material shortages
- Other workplace issues

Workers should provide a clear description of the problem.

The owner or responsible person can review the complaint and update its status.

Complaint statuses can include:
SUBMITTED
ASSIGNED
IN_PROGRESS
RESOLVED
OVERDUE
ESCALATED

Workers can track their complaints through the WorkVoice dashboard.

If a complaint remains unresolved beyond the configured period, it can become overdue.

An overdue complaint can be escalated by the worker through WorkVoice.

Workers should provide accurate information when submitting complaints.
