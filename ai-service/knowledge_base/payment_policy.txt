WorkVoice Payment Support Policy

Workers can raise a complaint if they have not received their expected salary or payment.

Common payment-related issues include:
- Salary not received
- Delayed payment
- Partial payment
- Incorrect payment
- Overtime payment not received
- Payment promised but not provided

Workers should provide clear information about the payment issue when raising a complaint.

A payment complaint should be reviewed by the responsible owner or supervisor.

The worker can track the complaint status through WorkVoice.

If the complaint remains unresolved and becomes overdue, the worker can escalate the complaint through WorkVoice.

Workers should keep relevant evidence such as payment records, messages, attendance records, or other documents when available.
