WorkVoice Leave Support Policy

Workers can raise a leave-related request when they need time away from work.

Common leave-related issues include:
- Request for personal leave
- Request for medical leave
- Emergency leave
- Leave request not reviewed
- Leave request not responded to

Workers should provide the reason and expected duration of leave when possible.

The responsible owner or supervisor should review the leave request.

Workers should follow the workplace's applicable leave procedures and communicate their request as early as possible when circumstances allow.

Workers can use WorkVoice to track the status of their request.
