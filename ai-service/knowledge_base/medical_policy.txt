WorkVoice Medical Support Policy

Workers can report workplace-related medical concerns through WorkVoice.

Common medical-related issues include:
- Workplace injury
- Illness affecting the ability to work
- Need for medical attention
- Medical emergency
- Unsafe conditions causing health concerns

Workers should provide a clear description of the medical issue when possible.

For serious or emergency medical situations, workers should seek appropriate emergency medical assistance immediately rather than relying only on the WorkVoice complaint system.

A medical-related complaint can be tracked through WorkVoice.

Workers should provide relevant medical documentation when required and available.
